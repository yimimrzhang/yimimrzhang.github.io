Research Interests

My research interests include nonparametric statistics, especially statistical inference based on ranked-set sampling, machine learning and deep learning applications, and multiple hypothesis testing. As a statistician, I am also interested in exploring new synergistic research collaborations with colleagues across Villanova and beyond.
